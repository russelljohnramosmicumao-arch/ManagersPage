Kape' Bar-Rio Manager — Employees Sub-tabs Fix

FIXED
Attendance and Schedule Builder were moved under Employees, but the old Workforce module was only initialized by the former top-level tabs.

This update makes:
Employees > Attendance
Employees > Schedule Builder
explicitly initialize KBRWorkforce.

RESULT
- Employee Information remains the default sub-tab.
- Attendance loads recorded hours correctly.
- Schedule Builder loads the week and schedule controls correctly.
- Duplicate to Next Week remains available.
- Inventory & Supply and Promo Control changes are preserved.

- inventory-add-item.js
- promo-discount-control.js
- workforce.js
- sw.js
