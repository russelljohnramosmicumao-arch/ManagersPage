Sales analysis

Completed transactions stay in your shared database. Dates and hours use Philippine time and the time the order was placed. Open and cancelled orders are excluded.

Cup stock estimate

Enter cups currently in stock. Days remaining = stock ÷ average cups used per calendar day in the selected period, including days with no sales. These stock entries stay on this device. Usage includes free drinks and Kuya John charges. Estimates assume one cup per drink and exclude waste; they are not connected to inventory yet.
